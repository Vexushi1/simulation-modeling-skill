# Sensitivity evidence

Use [H2](../../modules/08_model_verification.md) and its current source-reviewed contract.
Bind primary and participant H1/E receipts, the constant external input levels, observable,
units, finite-difference criterion and target claim. Report each original-unit difference
and slope against the declared primary. Primary slope and zero-baseline relative responses
are null with their mathematical reason. A finite OAT observation is not parameter
sensitivity or a global derivative. Preserve all finite points, failed bounds, scope and
support/modify/reject disposition.

Complete source-required studies provide local derivative, normalized sensitivity,
parameter OAT, Morris, Sobol S1/ST and optional S2, weighted full-subset finite ANOVA,
and PRCC. Bind the actual alternative-world permission, ordered factors/domain/law,
deterministic design/roles/seed, every point's numerical acceptance, metric units,
prior criteria and independent study receipt. State finite-stencil, grid, sampling or
discrete-law scope. Classic Sobol/ANOVA need independent product laws; correlated PRCC
is conditional association. Preserve constant-output/zero-reference/rank degeneracies
and every bootstrap replicate. Required missing or undefined studies block acceptance;
finite estimates outside [0,1] remain evidence, without clipping.
