Use [H2](../../modules/08_model_verification.md). Bind each model's actual C/D/E/H1,
the common frozen Problem, physical target, input, time, explicit observable mapping,
known units and prior metric criterion. Each advanced study required by the structural
claim must have a complete independently consumed study receipt; successful standalone
estimators do not replace the H2 claim decision. A source-bound review must identify material
current mathematical or physical differences and address equivalence. Different hashes,
variable names, parameters, Blocks or solvers alone are insufficient. Report saved-sample
metrics, actual deviations, scope and required return action on modify/reject. Neither
registered difference nor agreement establishes reality validity.
