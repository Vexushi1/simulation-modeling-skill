# Refinement and numerical obligation evidence

The [H1 module](../../modules/07_numerical_verification.md) defines the source-reviewed three-level assessment. Preserve actual ode4 integer grids and ode45/ode15s native adaptive meshes. Compare actual coarse points/endpoints or explicitly declared complete PWL reconstructions; never pair different adaptive indices or relabel interpolated evaluations as native samples.

Declare tolerances, contraction, floor, units, observation mapping, all five obligation decisions, executable checks, windows and budgets before reading results. Check the coarse primary and all three check results. Report residual/integrated defect, equality/inequality violation, prefix inventory/flux balance, event count/brackets/time agreement and detrended drift with their exact recorded scope. Preserve technically valid rejection separately from missing data, ambiguous events and mathematical incompleteness.

PWL maxima and signed/absolute/squared integrals describe the recorded reconstruction. True continuous-solution or exact-event conclusions require applicable source-bound, independently reviewed output/check/time/rate envelopes, and acceptance remains conditional on those bounds. Three-run agreement, formal solver names, success and an empirical order supply no error proof or physical validation.

Event records include each referenced actual observable's original indexed samples supporting the reconstructed crossing, with their raw units and interpolation rule. A denser unrelated union axis cannot hide a sparse event observation bracket. Full-window continuous claims also require the full reconstructed trajectory comparison. Legacy final metrics consume actual declared refinement points; recorded min/max compatibility keeps its limited comparison scope explicit.
